How to show this

Member page: open the map

That page is what a new person sees. Keep this page off the lobby screen.

Live camera

Phone, Safari or Chrome. Allow the camera. Whole body in the frame. Green lines mean it can see them. They hold still for a beat, or tap Keep this.

Order: stand, squat, hinge, arms up, sit to stand.

If the camera will not start

Open the member page with demo on:

index.html?demo=1

Then tap Skip camera. You still get a full sample map.

What they should feel

Not a score. Not a medical exam. A first hour that already knows how to scale them.

Video stays on the phone. Nothing uploads.
